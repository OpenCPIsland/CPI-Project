"I would use two directional lights when I baked the lighting. They were both directional lights that were pointing in the exact same positions and rotations.

Light 1:
Type: Directional
Color: FFEDCC
Mode: Baked
Intensity: 0.72
Indirect Multiplier: 0.44
Shadow Type: Soft Shadows
Baked Shadow Angle: 0.7


Light 2:
Type: Directional
Color: FFEDCC
Mode: Baked
Intensity: 0.92
Indirect Multiplier: 0.96
Shadow Type: Soft Shadows
Baked Shadow Angle: 7.0"

and

"You can make one with the same settings I used:

In Unity, in the project window, right click and go to Create > Material. Name it LightmapBakeSkybox. For this new material set the Shader type to Skybox > Procedural.

Here are the settings to use:
Sun: High Quality
Sun Size: 0.04
Sun Size Convergence: 5
Atmosphere Thickness: 1
Sky Tint: C915DE
Ground: FFEC96
Exposure: 1"
