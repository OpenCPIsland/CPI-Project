If you need to do other things or walk away, Unity will still import fine in the background. Just make sure to have sleep off in the Windows Settings to prevent cache corruption.

Roughly the import process can take anywhere from 15 minutes to an hour. Depending on your CPU and RAM. This is just the first import process when Unity caches the assets in the Library folder. After the first import, opening the project will take 30 seconds up to 2 minutes to open. This is a lot better than it was on Unity 2017... It took 12 hours to import during the caching process.
