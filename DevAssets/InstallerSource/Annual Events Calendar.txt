There is a party switcher in-game. It can be accessed via the debug menu under the Party Switcher. Evergreen means regular mode without a party and you can also force a certain party to be always active. There is also an option to automatically start the parties yearly. This is the existing party schedule:

Rainbow Migration: January 10th to February 1st
Blizzard Beach: February 1st to March 1st
Anniversary Party: March 8th to April 1st
April Fools: April 1st to April 8th

World Penguin Day: April 8th to May 1st
2017 -
Week 1: April 8th to April 19th
Week 2: April 19th to May 1st
2018: April 8th to May 1st

Radio Disney Music Awards RDMA: April 8th to May 1st
OpenCPI Anniversary: May 1st to May 8th

Pirate Expedition:
June 6th to June 27th

Summer Splashdown
Leadup: June 26th to July 4th
4th of July celebration: July 3rd to July 6th
Main Event: July 4th to August 8th

Medieval Party
2018 and 2026: August 15th to September 5th

Halloween
Leadup: September 23rd to September 30th
2018: October 1st to October 23rd
2026: October 24th to November 2nd
2025: November 3rd to November 7th

Holiday Party 2017 Construction: November 23rd to December 1st

Holiday Party 2026: December 1st to January 4th


All of these dates starts and end at 12:00am (midnight) UTC or also Penguin Standard Time in game which is also based on UTC.

There are also commands (they are more for testing purposes). The following commands can be typed into the in-game chat bar: !ac [CoinsAmount], !at [TubeDefID], and !ae [EquipmentDefID] to reward to the player's inventory.
